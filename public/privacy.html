Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026 • Service: HotMessExpress • Operated by: Michael Hebert

The short version

We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising. We use no third-party analytics or advertising trackers. Your household’s data is separated from other households’, and what each family member can see depends on their role. Children never see adult-only areas such as bills and finances.

1. Who we are

HotMessExpress (the “Service”) is a household-management app: chores, routines, shopping and pantry lists, schedules, family messages, bills, and tools for families with one or two households, including separated parents who share care of a child. It is operated by Michael Hebert, an individual based in Texas, United States (“we”, “us”).

Privacy questions and requests: michael@hotmessexpress.lol.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or email, your name, email address and profile picture, plus your role in the household (parent/admin, child, or pet) and which household you belong to.
  • Household content you add. Tasks and routines, shopping and pantry lists, meal plans, appointments, notes, messages between household members, activity and “quality time” entries, and similar items.
  • Children’s information. A parent or administrator creates a profile for each child (name, role, and any details the parent enters). See section 9.
  • Health-related information. Only what you choose to enter, such as medications, refill dates, and emotion or mood logs. This is sensitive, and it is visible only to the household members your role settings allow.
  • Financial information. Bills and expenses you enter. If you connect a bank through SimpleFIN Bridge, we receive read-only account and transaction data for that member. We never see your bank login, and we do not store payment-card numbers (payments are handled by Stripe).
  • Location and presence. If you use presence features, whether household members are home (from device signals or NFC tags you set up), and a home location you enter to show local weather. The Android app may request approximate location for this purpose.
  • Camera images and voice. If you scan a receipt or chore with the camera, the photo is sent to an AI image-reading service to extract text. Voice features use your device’s speech recognition, and spoken replies use Google text-to-speech.
  • Co-parenting information. Whether co-parenting mode is on, which household each parent belongs to, and each household’s address and phone number, which the other parent can see unless the household marks its address confidential.
  • School information. If a family member connects Google Classroom, course and assignment information from that account.
  • Smart-home information. If you connect Home Assistant or link Google Home, the connection details you provide, and device names and states so you can view and control your devices.
  • AI conversations. What you type to the built-in assistant, and the household context needed to answer (see section 6).
  • Technical data. Device and browser type, app version, error and performance logs, push-notification tokens, and IP address in server logs.

The app stores household data in your browser or device storage so it works quickly and offline; signing out clears that stored copy. We use only the storage the app needs to function, with no advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How we use information

To run the features you use; to keep the Service secure and prevent abuse; to provide support; to bill your subscription; to send notifications you enable; and to improve reliability. We do not use household content for advertising, and we do not sell it.

5. Bank data

If a household member connects a bank through SimpleFIN Bridge, only that member and a household superadmin can see the connection and the transactions it syncs. You can disconnect it at any time in the Finance area.

6. AI features

The assistant, daily briefings and image reading send your prompt and the household context needed to answer to AI providers acting as our service providers: Anthropic (Claude) and Google (Gemini and text-to-speech). A household administrator may instead supply their own AI keys. AI output can be wrong; it is not medical, legal, financial or custody advice.

8. Households and co-parenting

What a person sees depends on their role. Children do not see bills, finances or other adult-only areas, and some data is private to the member who created it. In co-parenting mode, two households connected as one family share only what is described here and in the app’s settings: each household’s address and phone number (unless marked confidential), and any items a parent explicitly chooses to share, which are visible to adult members only. Information from HotMessExpress is provided for convenience. It is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for a court order or a lawyer.

9. Children’s privacy

HotMessExpress is set up and controlled by parents. A child gets a profile that a parent creates, and can sign in with their own account only when a parent invites them. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child under 13 without a parent’s consent, which a parent gives by setting up or inviting the child. We do not show advertising to anyone. A parent can review, correct or delete a child’s information, or stop further collection, by emailing michael@hotmessexpress.lol.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Household data is stored in a multi-tenant database in the United States. Direct browser reads are limited by database access rules so an account can read only its own household, and our server functions also check household and role permissions in code. Gmail refresh tokens are encrypted at rest, and Google Home access tokens are stored only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure. If you find a problem, please tell us at michael@hotmessexpress.lol.

11. Retention and deletion

We keep information while your account is active. To have your household or account deleted, email michael@hotmessexpress.lol from the address on your account. We will delete or de-identify your information within 30 days, except where we must keep records for legal, tax or security reasons. Backups roll off on their normal schedule. You can also disconnect Gmail, Calendar and Classroom in Settings, disconnect a bank in the Finance area, and unlink Google Home in the Google Home app at any time.

12. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your information, and to disconnect any integration. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, and we will honor these requests wherever you live. Email michael@hotmessexpress.lol. We may need to verify who you are, and we will respond within 45 days. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

13. Changes to this policy

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
